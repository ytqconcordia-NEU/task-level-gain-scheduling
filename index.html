Method

Before each task, a neural network maps the initial tracking error and the features of the reference trajectory to the three parameters of a backstepping control law, which remain constant during the task. The neural network is trained without labeled data by backpropagation through time (BPTT), and the output layer restricts the parameters to a bounded admissible set. The experiments compare the proposed controller with three existing controllers (Controllers A, B, and C in the paper) in two scenarios.
